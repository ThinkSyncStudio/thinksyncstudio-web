Product policy

Privacy

Last updated September 6, 2026

The short version: Braglytics collects nothing. It has no account, sign-in, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs. The app developer does not receive anything you type.

What the app stores, and where

Player names, games, round scores, optional starting-card sums, who went out, scoring profiles, and settings are saved in the app’s own storage on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us because there is no developer-operated server to transmit it to.

We never receive your game data, cannot see it, and cannot recover it.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send. The export in the History tab creates a spreadsheet and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there—Files, Mail, AirDrop, or another app—is your choice, and that copy is governed by the service you select.

Braglytics makes no other network connections.

Data collected

None. Under Apple’s App Privacy definitions, Braglytics collects no data of any category and does not track you across apps or websites. The bundled privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

Permissions

Braglytics requests no access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location, or notifications.

Children

The planned App Store age rating is 4+. The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting and protecting your data

Deleting Braglytics removes its local data. Depending on your Apple backup settings, app data may also exist inside an iCloud or computer device backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us, and restoration is not guaranteed by the app.

Braglytics does not provide cloud sync or its own backup service. If your history matters to you, export a spreadsheet from the History tab before deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date. Any feature that changes how data is handled will be explained plainly.

Contact

Questions about Braglytics privacy can be sent to syncareal@icloud.com.
