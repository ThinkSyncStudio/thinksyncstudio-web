Privacy

Last updated September 6, 2026

ThinkSync Studio makes independent lifestyle apps. We aim to collect less data, explain what an app does with it, and keep product-specific details with the product they describe.

This website

This is a static informational website. It does not offer user accounts, accept payments, set first-party cookies, or include advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider may process routine technical data—such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and timestamp—to deliver and secure the site.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use that information to respond, provide support, and keep a reasonable record of the conversation. We do not sell it.

Our apps

Each ThinkSync Studio app may handle different information. A product’s own privacy notice controls how that product handles data and will be available from its listing or inside the app.

Braglytics

Braglytics stores game data on your device. It has no account, advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or developer-operated network service. You can export a spreadsheet when you want a portable copy. Your device platform may handle backups according to your settings and its own policies. Read the complete Braglytics Privacy Policy.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers to operate our website or email, and they process information only for those services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transaction.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support, recordkeeping, security, or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information in our possession. Rights vary by location and some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons. On-device app data can generally be managed in the app or by removing the app, as described in that product’s documentation.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Changes

We may update this notice as our studio and products change. The date above will show when this page was last revised.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@thinksyncstudio.com.
