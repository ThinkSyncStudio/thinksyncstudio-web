Resolve

Less scattered.
More prepared.

Repair records, invoices, emails, and unanswered questions can quickly pile up. Give your dispute a home and work through it one step at a time.

01

Put the facts in order

Collect supporting documents and build a timeline so the details are easier to find and follow.

02

Prepare your response

Draft correspondence using your case information. You review the details, choose what to say, and send it yourself.

03

Keep the next step in view

Track correspondence, deadlines, and escalation plans without losing the thread of your case.

Practical perspective

Decide what is worth pursuing.

Consider the evidence, effort, and potential costs before taking another step. AutoJustice’s Pursuit Score is a decision aid with factors and caveats—not a prediction of winning.

AutoJustice is self-help software, not a law firm. It does not provide legal advice or predict court outcomes.
